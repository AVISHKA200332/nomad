# Avenora - Website Pages Specification

## Overview
Avenora features five primary public-facing pages designed to provide a comprehensive and captivating journey for tourists.

---

### 1. Home (`/`)
- **Controller:** `HomeController.php`
- **View:** `app/Views/pages/home.php`
- **Styles / Scripts:** `home.css`, `main.js`
- **Purpose:** Primary landing gateway for prospective travelers.
- **Key Sections (Future):**
  - Hero banner with inspiring travel tagline and search/cta.
  - Featured / popular destinations carousel or grid.
  - Curated travel packages and special promotions.
  - Why Choose Avenora (value propositions, safety, expertise).
  - Testimonials and trust badges.
  - Newsletter / call to inquiry.

---

### 2. Destinations (`/destinations`)
- **Controller:** `DestinationController.php`
- **View:** `app/Views/pages/destination.php`
- **Styles / Scripts:** `destination.css`, `modules/destinations.js`
- **Purpose:** Showcase available travel locations, scenic wonders, and tourist hubs.
- **Key Sections (Future):**
  - Destination search and region filter bar.
  - Destination card grid with thumbnail, region, rating, and brief overview.
  - Pagination or dynamic category sorting.

---

### 3. Packages (`/packages`)
- **Controller:** `PackageController.php`
- **View:** `app/Views/pages/package.php`
- **Styles / Scripts:** `package.css`, `modules/packages.js`
- **Purpose:** Display structured tour packages, vacation itineraries, and pricing tiers.
- **Key Sections (Future):**
  - Package filtering by duration, budget, and destination type.
  - Detailed package cards featuring itinerary highlights, pricing, and inquiry buttons.
  - Frequently asked booking questions.

---

### 4. About (`/about`)
- **Controller:** `AboutController.php`
- **View:** `app/Views/pages/about.php`
- **Styles / Scripts:** `about.css`
- **Purpose:** Build trust, convey brand mission, history, and tourism expertise.
- **Key Sections (Future):**
  - Company story and vision for authentic travel experiences.
  - Core values (sustainability, customer satisfaction, local community support).
  - Guide/leadership highlights and industry certifications.

---

### 5. Contact (`/contact`)
- **Controller:** `ContactController.php`
- **View:** `app/Views/pages/contact.php`
- **Styles / Scripts:** `contact.css`
- **Purpose:** Provide accessible channels for travelers to reach out, consult, and submit inquiries.
- **Key Sections (Future):**
  - Interactive contact and custom itinerary inquiry form.
  - Direct contact details (phone, email, operating hours, office location).
  - Location map placeholder and emergency travel assistance notes.
